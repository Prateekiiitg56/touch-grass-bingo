Touch grass bingo

Download the photo checker while you have Wi-Fi. It is about 150 MB and lets the game work with no signal.

How to play

Walk somewhere green and keep the phone in your pocket. When you spot something on the card, tap its square and take one photo. Fill a whole row, column or diagonal to win.

The middle square is never free. Put your hand on real grass and photograph it.

Card settings

Friends who enter the same code get the same card.

Photos are checked on this phone by CLIP, an open-weight model, running in Transformers.js. No photo is uploaded.
